  The battery, LiFePO4 24V 25Ah, was chosen based on the motors. Our wormgear motors, 24V 60W DC brushed, were chosen for controlled, high torque
output for the sake of offroading and lenghtened operation time. Based on AntonLofgrens calculations the UGV is designed for 10-15 km/h speeds with these 
components, taking into account stalltorque,-current, size, energy consumption and budget. This is acceptable for the purposes of a demo platform and reliability.
DC-DC buckconverter for 24V IN and 5V 3A OUT conversion is planned to be implemented so that two rasperrypi 5's can be powered from the battery.

  The BTS7960 drivers that are rated for 24V and 42A which allow backdriving of the motors are, as of date, going to be used due to their reliability and online
suggestions, both AI (copilot, chatgpt 5) and forum discussions. Their cheap pricetag allows us to also test other plausible drivers without major costs if need be.
We considered implementing all four drivers need for each motor into a single board along with power cut-off, fuse protection and DC-DC buckconverer for the
Rasperry pi 5's. Due to time and scope constraints, I am proposing individual component implementation for the intial goal of building a fully functional system.

  Two rasperry pi 5's were bought: one for navigation and motor control and one for turret operation and target image processing. Leo6661 and Sofii-alt are 
responsible for code, software and navigation implementation on them.
